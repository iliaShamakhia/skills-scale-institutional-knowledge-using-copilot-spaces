# OctoAcme Project Management Process Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured yet flexible project management approach designed around five core principles:

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme's project management framework is built on a rigorous, phased lifecycle that emphasizes alignment, quality, and continuous improvement. The process begins with **Project Initiation**, where new ideas are validated through a lightweight one-pager that confirms business need, identifies stakeholders, and establishes success metrics. Once approved by sponsors and leadership, projects move into **Planning**, where the scope is broken into shippable increments, acceptance criteria are defined, dependencies are mapped, and a release timeline is established.

Execution and delivery are coordinated through a structured team rhythm and rigorous quality standards. Teams use GitHub Projects to track progress through daily standups and weekly delivery syncs. Pull requests follow strict conventions and undergo automated testing, linting, and security scanning before approval. Quality assurance is built into every step: unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and manual QA for feature acceptance. OctoAcme operates with three core roles—Project Managers (who coordinate schedules and risks), Product Managers (who define outcomes and prioritize work), and Developers (who implement features)—with structured communication cadences that ensure alignment across stakeholders.

Finally, **Release & Deployment** and **Retrospectives & Continuous Improvement** close the loop. Releases follow standardized checklists with pre-deployment verifications, staged testing, and rollback plans. Every sprint or milestone concludes with a blameless retrospective that identifies what went well, what could improve, and creates tracked action items. This end-to-end discipline ensures OctoAcme delivers reliable software predictably while building institutional knowledge and team resilience.

## Project Lifecycle

Every OctoAcme project follows these phases:

1. **Initiation** → Validate business need, align stakeholders, create project one-pager
2. **Planning** → Break work into shippable increments, identify risks and dependencies
3. **Execution** → Build, test, review, iterate with daily standups and weekly syncs
4. **Release** → Deploy to production with pre-release checklist and rollback planning
5. **Close & Retrospective** → Capture learnings and convert to improvements
